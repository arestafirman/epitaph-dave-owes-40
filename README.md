# Dave Owes Me $40, Plus Interest Since 2019

An epitaph for the Famous Last Words competition: a dying man's final words that settle his family, his bank, and one unpaid debt — Dave still owes him $40, plus interest since 2019.

## Entry

> Tell the kids I love them. Tell the bank I never will. And tell Dave he still owes me $40, plus interest since 2019.

## How it works

Run `node main.ts` and it writes the one-line epitaph (116 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
